How to read it:
  1. On the Mac, tap one frequency (e.g. 18.0) and hold it — don't use Auto.
  2. The Live peak here should jump to that frequency and a green pill appears.
  3. Now try 19.0, then 20.0. If the Live peak stops rising and sticks (e.g. at 18.5), that number is this phone's ceiling.
  4. Stop the tone — the Live peak should fall back to "—". If bands stay green with no tone playing, that's noise, not a real tone.
